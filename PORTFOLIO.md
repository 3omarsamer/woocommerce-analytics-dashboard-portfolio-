## LinkedIn post — no business brand name

Built a custom WooCommerce analytics dashboard to solve a real reporting challenge after an e-commerce platform migration. 📊

The goal was to combine a clean, Shopify-inspired reporting experience with reliable WooCommerce financial data and GA4 traffic insights.

The most valuable part wasn't the dashboard design — it was the engineering process: tracing inflated order totals to excluded statuses, reconciling revenue across reports, designing product classification rules, distinguishing GA4 event counts from sessions, and testing each milestone before release.

The project uses PHP, WordPress/WooCommerce, JavaScript, the GA4 Data API and HPOS-compatible reporting. Development was AI-assisted; I led requirements, workflow design, debugging, acceptance testing and release decisions.

## Suggested safe screenshots

1. Dashboard overview: KPI cards, date selector, charts; use synthetic or obscured business amounts.
2. Payment and customer reports: anonymized order/customer data.
3. Category and brand reports: mask any proprietary product/brand information you do not want indexed.
4. GA4 sessions/funnel: synthetic data or explicitly labeled historical aggregate test data.
5. Best Sellers / Best-Selling Sizes: use demo products and no live inventory values.
6. Order drill-down modal: **never** show real names, email addresses, phone numbers, shipping addresses or order identifiers.

## Why the project was built — concise portfolio statement

Following a Shopify-to-WooCommerce migration, the business needed richer, easier-to-read reporting for a **multi-brand sportswear catalog with thousands of SKUs and variations**. I led the requirements for a Shopify-inspired WordPress analytics dashboard that combined WooCommerce sales truth with Google Analytics 4 reporting through Google Cloud, plus tailored performance analysis by brand, category, gender, best-selling products and sizes. I directed iterative testing and debugging of revenue reconciliation, customer reporting and data classification using AI-assisted development.
